Orchestration and parallelism are already available. The question is whether a restricted action language, trusted contracts, and diagnostic-guided repair can make action generation more reliable.

Secondary hypothesis · smaller planning models

Smaller model→Same contract gate→Bounded repair→Execute, escalate, or stop

Reduced planning burden may enable comparable reliability at lower total cost and latency. Retries and escalation can erase savings. This hypothesis is unvalidated; automatic escalation is not implemented.

01Central hypothesis

Restricted programs plus verifiable contracts and diagnostic-guided repair may improve action reliability over unconstrained delegation.

02Bounded guarantees

Acceptance establishes only encoded checks. It does not guarantee intent alignment, factual truth, or external success.

03Existing foundation

Graph/schema validation and bounded repair exist. State contracts, requirement coverage, and richer effect policies remain proposed work.
